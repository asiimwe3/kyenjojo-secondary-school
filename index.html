About the School

A Community School in Kyenjojo Town

Kyenjojo Secondary School is a secondary school located in Kyenjojo town, the headquarters of Kyenjojo District in western Uganda.

Our mission is simple: give the young people of Kyenjojo a solid secondary education in a safe, supportive environment, and prepare them to pass their national examinations with confidence.

Parents and guardians are always welcome at the school office to discuss their children's progress, fees and admission.

Academics

Learning That Prepares
for UNEB Examinations

We follow the Uganda national secondary school curriculum, preparing our students for the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) examinations.

01

Ordinary Level (S.1 – S.4)

A four-year programme leading to the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE).

  • English Language & Literature
  • Mathematics
  • Sciences: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Social studies: History, Geography
  • Languages & Entrepreneurship
02

ICT & Computer Studies

Students build practical computing skills for the modern world, from basics to office applications.

  • Computer literacy
  • Office applications
  • ICT for UNEB coursework
03

Discipline & Values

We believe discipline and respect are the foundation of academic success. Our teachers work closely with students and parents.

  • Respect and responsibility
  • Guidance and counselling
  • Parent – teacher engagement
04

Co-curricular Activities

Education beyond the classroom keeps students active, healthy and confident.

  • Sports and athletics
  • Clubs and societies
  • Debate and music
Admissions

How to Apply

Joining Kyenjojo Secondary School is straightforward. Follow these steps, or call the school office and we will guide you.

1

Get an application form

Visit the school office in Kyenjojo town to collect the application form, or call +256 773 159351 to enquire.

2

Bring your results

S.1 applicants bring their PLE result slip. Transferring students bring report cards and a transfer letter from their previous school.

3

Interview & placement

The school will confirm placement and inform you of the requirements for your class.

4

Pay fees & report

The school office will share the current fee structure and term dates so you can plan and report on time.
